FAQ

Common questions

Can vinyl flooring go over ceramic tiles?

Yes, as long as the existing floor is solid and level. Deep grout lines need to be filled with levelling compound first.

How long does a bedroom redesign take?

For a 10 to 15 m² room with electrics, ceiling, paint, flooring and joinery, 5 to 8 weeks is realistic. Custom joinery usually has the longest lead time.

Can you recommend the trades you hired?

Yes. Send a message with your area and the type of work, and I will share their details.

Can I use your budget and schedule templates?

Of course. Ask through the form and I will send the Excel / Google Sheets templates.
